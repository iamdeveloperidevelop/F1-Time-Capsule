## Conflict log

1. **Williams drivers (mid-Dec vs 18 Dec):** `MS-1982-01` and `MS-1981-12-48` leave Williams without a settled race pair (retirements / testing only). `UPI-WILLIAMS-1981` (18 Dec) confirms Reutemann–Rosberg. **Resolution for drafting:** use UPI for confirmed 1982 pair; cite MS pieces for organisation, Head, sponsors, six-wheeler R&D, and the earlier unsettled state if narrating chronology.
2. **SA GP date in PRE-01 (“23rd” January) vs season metadata (21 January):** do not resolve in teams rewrite; already `1982-R01-CUT-02`.
3. **McLaren car naming:** UPI mentions MP4/1B with future Porsche; PRE-01 stresses DFV meantime and Porsche for 1983. Treat Porsche/MP4/1B as announced programme, not confirmed first-session race package (`1982-ENT-02`).
4. **Alfa Romeo drivers:** these existing sources do not support a confirmed 1982 pair — keep gap, do not import later knowledge.
